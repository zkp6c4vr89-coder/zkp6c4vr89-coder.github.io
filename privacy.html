Privacy Policy

last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers everything called ASCII Studio: the website and web studio (zkp6c4vr89-coder.github.io), the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and others), and the desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux). It is made by Nandhu.

The short version: ASCII Studio does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers and no ads.

Your photos, videos and camera

  • Images and videos you open are turned into ASCII art on your own device, using your computer's graphics card. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The camera is only used while you switch it on, only to draw the live ASCII preview on your screen. Nothing is recorded unless you press Record, and recordings are saved straight to your own device.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (wallpaper choice, colours, clock style and so on) and any picture you set as a wallpaper are stored locally in your browser or app, and never leave your device.
  • You can delete them at any time by removing the extension, clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app.

Website access (browser extension)

When you right-click an image on a site that doesn't share its files, the extension asks permission to read files from that one site, only to load the image you chose. It's optional, asked for only when needed, and you can remove it any time in your browser's extension settings.

Updates (desktop app)

The desktop app checks GitHub (github.com, owned by GitHub, Inc.) every few hours to see whether a newer version exists, and downloads it from there. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address when this happens; ASCII Studio sends nothing else and receives only the release information and files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Publishing tools

The developer uses Google's Chrome Web Store API and Mozilla's add-ons API only to upload new versions of the extension to those stores. These tools act on the developer's own store accounts and never access any user's data.

Fonts on this website

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when the page loads. The studio, extension and app themselves use fonts already on your device.

Children

ASCII Studio collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
